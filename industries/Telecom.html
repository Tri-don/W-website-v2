Connectivity demands more resilient operations and better experiences.

Telecom operators must keep networks reliable, connect complex operational systems, and deliver customer experiences that encourage loyalty. Infrastructure disruptions, customer churn, and growing cybersecurity demands put pressure on both operations and profitability.

18,000+

Infrastructure theft and vandalism incidents disrupted communications services in the U.S. in 2025.

These incidents affected approximately 11.8 million customers, highlighting the operational and societal costs of network disruption.

Source: USTelecom, 2026 report on critical communications infrastructure.

77%

Of consumers may feel no loyalty to their telecom provider.

Deloitte's 2026 industry outlook highlights the challenge operators face in differentiating their services and retaining customers.

Source: Deloitte, 2026 Telecommunications Industry Outlook. Reported as an upper estimate.

$15–19B

Estimated annual spending on core cybersecurity activities by mobile network operators.

Cybersecurity demands create a substantial and growing investment requirement for telecom operators.

Source: GSMA, The Impact of Cybersecurity Regulation on Mobile Network Operators, November 2025.
