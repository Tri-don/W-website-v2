Energy markets leave little room for operational blind spots.

Market volatility, grid constraints, and increasing cyber threats put pressure on energy operations and commodity trading. Fragmented systems and limited visibility make it harder to manage exposure, coordinate operations, and respond to change.

Record single-day trading range for WTI crude oil futures in March 2026.

A sharp intraday price movement highlights how quickly commodity markets can shift, increasing the importance of timely pricing, exposure visibility, and risk management.

Source: CME Group, What History Reveals About Today's Crude Oil Volatility, June 2026. This refers to the front-month WTI futures contract on March 9, 2026.

2,500+ GW

Of renewable, storage, and large-load projects remain stalled in grid connection queues worldwide.

Growing electricity demand and renewable generation are adding pressure to existing grid infrastructure and integration processes.

Source: International Energy Agency, Electricity 2026.

3×

Cyberattacks on energy utilities have tripled in the past four years.

Increasingly sophisticated cyber threats are raising the stakes for secure systems, infrastructure visibility, and operational resilience.

Source: International Energy Agency, Energy and AI, 2025.
