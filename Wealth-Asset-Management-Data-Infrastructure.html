Building a trusted foundation for investment data.

Wealth & Asset Management Data Infrastructure.

Better investment decisions start with trusted data.

Wealth and asset management firms rely on data from portfolio systems, custodians, market data providers, fund administrators, and other sources. When information is fragmented or inconsistent, reconciling portfolios, producing reliable reports, and maintaining a consistent view of investments becomes more complex.

From portfolio accounting and alternative asset data to investment reporting and analytics, Wissen helps wealth and asset management firms connect fragmented data, modernize legacy platforms, and build reliable infrastructure for investment operations.
